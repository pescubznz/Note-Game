WELCOME, WANDERER

Adventure with purpose.

Walk with A / D or the arrow keys. Press Space to jump over terrain and enemies, and F to swing your sword. Touching an enemy costs a heart; defeating one restores a heart.

Every 180 meters, a village offers a safe resting place. Approach its scriptorium and press E to write your class notes. You can read your journal anywhere; writing requires a scriptorium.

Forest, desert, and snowy terrain unfold from your world's seed. Progress and notes save automatically in this browser. Export a backup from Worlds to move to another device.
