WELCOME, WANDERER

Adventure with purpose.

Move with A / D or the arrow keys. Hold Space for a higher jump, or tap it for a short hop. In lakes, hold Space to paddle and jump out of the water.

Hold P to fire your rifle in the direction you face. Press E to roll on land: you dodge enemy damage for 0.28 seconds, with a 1.1-second cooldown between rolls. Touching an enemy costs a heart; defeating one restores a heart.

Villages are roughly 1,000 meters apart, with distances varying by seed. Approach a scriptorium door and press R to read or write class notes. Your journal can only be opened at a scriptorium. Villages restore health and set your respawn point.

Explore forests, deserts, snowy peaks, wetlands, and volcanic highlands, with mountain trails and swimmable lakes. Existing notes and distance are preserved when the terrain updates. Worlds save in this browser; use Worlds → Export to keep a backup.
